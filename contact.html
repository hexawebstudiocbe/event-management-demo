Frequently Asked Questions

How early should we book your services?

For large events like weddings, we recommend booking us 6-8 months in advance. For corporate events or smaller parties, a 2-3 month notice is usually sufficient depending on the season.

Can you organize destination weddings?

Yes! We specialize in destination weddings across South India (Ooty, Kodaikanal, Mahabalipuram, Kerala) and international destinations as well. We handle all logistics, travel, and accommodation for your guests.

Do you provide catering and decoration?

Absolutely. Aura Events is an end-to-end event management company. We have an in-house decor team and partner with the finest premium caterers in Tamil Nadu to provide authentic and multi-cuisine menus.
